# One number moved on 2026-09-18. The other three did not.

On 2026-09-18, On Holding AG signed Kylian Mbappé. That day On's public mentions ran at **8.74 times** its baseline, while the other 11 running-shoe brands all sat between 0.73 and 1.08. Seven weeks later, AI named On **zero times** across 20 football questions, and four times said Mbappé wears Nike. Mentions moved. Nothing else did.

A brand reports on four things at once: search share, social mentions, AI mentions, direct traffic. Four units, four denominators, four questions. Read one alone and you get a confident wrong answer.

## The four numbers answer four different questions

**Search share** counts slots. When someone searches this category, how many of the results are yours?

**Social mentions** count the people who already know you. How many will open your page on their own?

**AI mentions** count what a model has in mind. Ask a model a question and see whether you get named.

**Direct traffic** counts memory. How many type your address without going through search, social, or AI?

None substitutes for another. Merge them into one "brand health" score and you are adding numbers with different units, denominators, and time spans. When the score moves, you still cannot say which line moved.

## On Holding AG, measured on all four lines

The brand under measurement is **On Holding AG** (NYSE: ONON), the Swiss running-shoe maker. That day it named Mbappé global brand ambassador and appointed Thierry Henry as football director.

**Direct traffic is almost always the one line you cannot get.** It lives inside the brand's own analytics tool. The other three anyone can look up, so they get compared against the fourth, producing a verdict on traffic you never measured. Leaving that cell empty beats an estimate.

## Three questions to ask once the lines sit side by side

**Which line moved first.** Sequence gives causality somewhere to stand. Only social mentions can fix an order in time, because it has daily granularity. Search share and AI mentions are same-day snapshots with no timeline, and direct traffic cannot be fetched at all. **Put one line with time granularity next to three without it and readers read side by side as simultaneous.**

**Whether the directions agree.** Social mentions rose by 8.74x. Search share and AI mentions did not move. **Divergent directions are themselves the finding.** When all four rise, the category is rising; when one rises, the company did something.

**Whether one line substitutes for another.** One up and another down can mean one took from the other, or that both rose. Nike ran at **0.96** that day; Mbappé's old club dipped slightly.

## Where a single line misleads

**Read only search share** and the conclusion is "On solidly holds the top tier of running-shoe search." Not wrong: 4 slots, tied with Altra, ASICS, and HOKA. But it misses that On has entered football while search shows nothing. In search, On looks like **a brand with no football business**.

**Read only social mentions** and the conclusion is "On's brand heat changed completely after 2026-09-18." Wrong. Seven weeks later pageviews are back at the 36,555 norm, 26 views from September's average of 36,529. **A peak is not a waterline.** Mentions alone turn one day into a new normal.

**Read only AI mentions** and the conclusion is "On ranks 8th in AI, the opportunity is big." You see none of On scoring zero across the 20 football questions. **Worse, the four "Mbappé wears Nike" answers tell you two things at once**: that On ranks 8th in AI, and that AI knows nothing about On's biggest signing. Both sit in the same dataset; reading one hides the other.

**The four together tell the story.** Social mentions moved on 2026-09-18 by 8.74x, and the peer comparison shows this was not a category-wide move. Search share and AI mentions did not move between 2026-09-18 and 2026-11-09. Direct traffic cannot be fetched, and the nearest figure (Q2 DTC +26%) predates the event. **So the answer to "which line moved" is: social mentions moved, for three and a half days.** Anyone claiming this data shows "On has taken off on the AI side" has to name the date their numbers came from.

**One line alone gives you that line's story. Four together tell you which is true.**
